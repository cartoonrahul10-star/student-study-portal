Important concepts for B.Tech students.

Important Topics

1. Coherence:

Coherence is the property of light waves having a constant phase relationship.

2. Coherence Length:

Coherence length is the distance over which a light wave maintains its coherence.

3. Coherence Time:

Coherence time is the time interval over which a light wave maintains its coherence.

4. Numerical Aperture:

NA = √(n₁² − n₂²)

5. Critical Angle:

sin C = n₂ / n₁
